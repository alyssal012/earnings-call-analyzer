Dana Whitcomb -- Vice President, Investor Relations
Thank you, operator. Joining me today are Marcus Feld, our Chief Executive Officer, and Priya Raman, our Chief Financial Officer. Remarks today may include forward-looking statements that involve risks and uncertainties.

Marcus Feld -- Chief Executive Officer
Thanks, Dana. The third quarter was a challenging environment for the industry. Revenue grew 3% to $806 million, which was below our expectations. We saw softness in wholesale as several partners became more cautious with orders, and traffic in our stores was softer than we anticipated in September. That said, our direct-to-consumer business grew 9%, and our loyalty program reached 6.5 million members. Trail footwear remained a bright spot, although growth moderated. We are taking actions to manage inventory and protect the brand. We believe the long-term opportunity remains intact, but we are being prudent given the uncertain consumer backdrop and continued headwinds in wholesale.

Priya Raman -- Chief Financial Officer
Thank you, Marcus. Gross margin declined 60 basis points to 46.1%, primarily due to higher promotional activity to clear seasonal inventory. Operating income decreased 8% to $97 million. Diluted earnings per share were $1.08 compared to $1.15 a year ago. Inventory ended the quarter at $702 million, up 15%, which is higher than we would like relative to sales. Free cash flow was $31 million. Given the softer demand and wholesale headwinds, we are lowering our full-year guidance. We now expect revenue growth of 5% to 6%, down from our prior outlook of 9% to 10%, and earnings per share of $4.55 to $4.70. We are pausing share repurchases for the remainder of the year.
